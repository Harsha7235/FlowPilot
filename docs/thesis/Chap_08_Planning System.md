# Chapter 8 – Planning System

## 8.1 Introduction

The Planning System is responsible for transforming high-level engineering requirements into structured engineering execution plans. It acts as the decision-making layer between the user interface and the engineering execution engines.

Rather than immediately generating GNU Radio flowgraphs from natural language prompts, the Planning System first analyzes the engineering problem, identifies requirements, determines engineering constraints, retrieves relevant knowledge, and constructs a structured execution strategy.

This planning-first approach improves engineering accuracy, reduces generation errors, enables explainable decision making, and supports autonomous multi-step engineering workflows.

The Planning System therefore represents the cognitive reasoning layer of the FlowPilot platform.

---

## 8.2 Design Objectives

The Planning System has been designed to achieve the following objectives.

- Understand engineering intent.
- Extract engineering requirements.
- Identify engineering constraints.
- Decompose complex engineering problems.
- Retrieve relevant engineering knowledge.
- Select appropriate engineering capabilities.
- Produce structured engineering execution plans.
- Support explainable engineering reasoning.
- Coordinate downstream engineering engines.

---

## 8.8 Intent Analyzer

### 8.8.1 Introduction

The Intent Analyzer is the first processing component of the FlowPilot Planning System. Its primary responsibility is to understand the engineering objective expressed in a user's natural language request and transform it into a structured representation that can be processed by downstream planning components.

Engineering requests submitted to FlowPilot may vary significantly in wording, complexity, and level of technical detail. Some users may provide complete engineering specifications, while others may describe only the desired outcome.

The Intent Analyzer bridges this gap by identifying the engineering objective without requiring users to understand GNU Radio terminology or Digital Signal Processing (DSP) concepts.

The output produced by the Intent Analyzer serves as the foundation for every subsequent planning operation.

---

### 8.8.2 Design Objectives

The Intent Analyzer has been designed to achieve the following objectives.

- Understand natural language engineering requests.
- Identify engineering goals.
- Detect engineering domains.
- Recognize communication systems.
- Identify SDR hardware.
- Detect engineering constraints.
- Produce structured intent representations.
- Support explainable planning.

## 8.10 Constraint Analyzer

The Constraint Analyzer is responsible for identifying, validating, and managing all engineering constraints that influence the planning and execution of an engineering project. While the Requirement Extractor determines what the system must accomplish, the Constraint Analyzer determines the conditions under which those requirements must be satisfied.

Engineering constraints may originate from user specifications, hardware capabilities, software compatibility, runtime limitations, engineering standards, or project-specific requirements. These constraints guide every downstream planning decision and ensure that generated engineering solutions remain feasible, executable, and reliable.

The Constraint Analyzer therefore acts as the engineering feasibility evaluator of the FlowPilot Planning System.

## 8.13 Capability Mapper

### 8.13.1 Introduction

The Capability Mapper is responsible for translating engineering tasks into the engineering capabilities required for successful execution. Rather than mapping tasks directly to specific GNU Radio blocks, the Capability Mapper identifies the functional capabilities needed to accomplish each engineering task and delegates capability resolution to the Knowledge System.

This capability-driven approach decouples engineering planning from implementation details. As a result, FlowPilot remains compatible with future GNU Radio releases, Out-of-Tree (OOT) modules, custom DSP libraries, and emerging SDR technologies without requiring modifications to the Planning System.

The Capability Mapper therefore acts as the abstraction layer between engineering planning and engineering implementation.

---

### 8.13.2 Design Objectives

The Capability Mapper has been designed to:

- Translate engineering tasks into engineering capabilities.
- Eliminate hardcoded block mappings.
- Support capability-based engineering reasoning.
- Enable automatic discovery of implementation alternatives.
- Improve planner flexibility.
- Support future GNU Radio versions.
- Enable autonomous engineering adaptation.

## 8.18 Planner Service APIs

## 8.19 Complete Planning Workflow

### 8.19.1 Introduction

The Complete Planning Workflow describes the end-to-end planning lifecycle performed by the FlowPilot Planning System. It illustrates how natural language engineering requests are transformed into structured Engineering Execution Plans through multiple coordinated planning stages.

Unlike conventional AI systems that immediately generate implementation artifacts, FlowPilot performs systematic engineering planning before project generation begins. This planning process integrates engineering knowledge, engineering memory, engineering context, engineering constraints, available resources, and engineering reasoning into a single execution strategy.

The Planning Workflow therefore represents the cognitive engineering process of the FlowPilot platform.

---

### 8.19.2 Overall Planning Workflow

The complete planning workflow is illustrated below.
